# Tile obtainability audit — 16 September 2026

Checked all 52 completion tiles and the Witching Hour bonus tile against the OSRS Wiki's live boss, item, and activity pages. The free space requires no reward. This audit checks obtainability and source accuracy, not whether a team is likely to finish every grind during October. Quest, skill, task and equipment access still apply.

## Findings to resolve before the event

- **Royal Titans:** both prayer scrolls stop dropping once read. A team whose members all have the prayers cannot complete the current all-four rule. Recommended revision: require both staff crowns only. This gameplay change has not been applied without the organiser's choice. [Royal Titans](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Royal_Titans)
- **Lumberjack outfit:** the four pieces are legitimate Temple Trekking rewards, but outfit duplicate protection means participants who already own the outfit need an eligibility check before relying on fresh drops. This audit does not verify a storage workaround or recommend deleting existing gear. [Lumberjack outfit](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Lumberjack_outfit)
- **Mimic:** the 5-completion path works; “rare clue-table reward” has no explicit item whitelist. Decide whether the alternative means a Ring of 3rd age, 3rd-age equipment, other clue uniques, and whether it must come from a Mimic casket. [The Mimic](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Mimic)
- **Spooky Wardrobe:** now requires completing the Halloween holiday event. The Skeleton outfit and qualifying-drop alternative has been removed; completion depends on the seasonal event being available.
- **Zombie Apocalypse:** now requires a Pest Control win with every participant wearing a zombie-themed outfit. The poisoned raid alternative has been removed. Organisers should specify which outfits qualify.
- **Difficulty, not impossibility:** Skotizo's required uncut onyx is 1/1,000 per kill with a totem needed per attempt. Vorkath also requires a necklace and a rare jar/pet/visage. Their quantities and completion paths were left intact. [Skotizo](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Skotizo), [Vorkath](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Vorkath)

## Corrections made

Corrected the Pharaoh's Curse source to Tombs of Amascut, the Undertaker's location to distinguish Mort'ton from Otto's Grotto, and the Grim Reaper's travel wording to “Visit”. Specified Ancient Pyramid mummies for the ancient staff. Removed stale crafted-item names from Bandos and Grotesque Guardians metadata. Added requirement notes explaining Sire's Unsired redemption, eligible ToB modes, Inquisitor/Oathplate piece categories, Dossier and Zamorakian spear names. Existing submission choice IDs, quantities, and AND/OR rules were preserved.
